Manifesto

Craft over
shortcuts.

V01 was built on a simple idea: the systems should serve the work — not the other way around.

We make commercials, films, music videos, and VFX. The AI and pipelines we build inside the studio exist to help us tell better stories, faster, with more control. They are tools. The work is the point.

We hire filmmakers who care about frames and engineers who care about taste. We train on data we own or have license to. We finish to broadcast standards, every time.

Cinematic storytelling. Visual craft. Emotional impact. Built for what's next.

What We Believe
01

The Frame is the Product

We're not in the business of selling tools or showing off models. The output is a finished frame, on screen, in front of an audience.

02

Human Direction, Always

Directors and artists drive every decision. AI accelerates them. It does not replace taste, story, or judgment.

03

Owned Data, No Shortcuts

Our models train on licensed, owned, or fully synthetic data. Provenance is a feature, not a footnote.

04

Iterate in Public

We share what we learn from real productions — what shipped, what didn't, what we're still figuring out.

05

Hollywood Standards

Compositing, finishing, and delivery built to the same bar feature pipelines hold themselves to.

06

Long-Term Partners

We say no to fit-for-the-moment work. We build relationships with people we want to make a body of work with.
