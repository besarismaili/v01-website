Lab Principles
01

Craft First

The system serves the work. If a tool doesn't make the final frame better, it doesn't ship.

02

Owned Data

Models are trained on licensed, owned, or fully synthetic data. No web-scraped shortcuts.

03

Human-Led

Directors and artists drive every decision. AI is collaborator, never author.

04

Ship to Learn

Tools live on real productions before they're called done. Theory is for slides.
