The Brief

A film about
memory.

Atlas Pictures came to V01 with a question: what does memory feel like, in a world where it can be archived, replayed, and rewritten?

The brief was short — a four-minute cinematic film, no dialogue, that could anchor a brand campaign across theatrical, streaming, and social. The look had to feel inevitable, not invented. The execution had to ship in 11 weeks.

"We didn't want a film about AI. We wanted a film that could only be made because of it."

We treated this as a directing problem first, an engineering problem second. Mara Voss led the script and visual treatment. The lab built the toolchain that let us iterate without breaking the look.

Technique · How It Was Built

Look Development

The film's visual language was developed in Atlas — V01's owned-data character and environment model — trained on a private reference set commissioned for the project.

  • Atlas v3 fine-tune · 6,400 reference frames
  • Lookdev: 3 weeks · 2 directors of photography
  • Lighting referenced from on-set HDRIs captured during scout

Previz to Plate

Previz Engine generated real-time camera moves and rough shot blocks. Live Capture kept on-set framing aligned to previz, frame-accurate.

  • 87 previz shots · 214 final shots
  • Six shoot days · two unit · virtual + live action
  • Synced via Ledger Pipeline — no shot left unversioned

Compositing

Stack Compositor handled matte extraction, AI-assisted cleanup, and look matching across plates. Nuke 15 for hero shots and final assembly.

  • 214 final composites
  • 43 hero shots fully assembled in Nuke
  • Render Mesh: hybrid GPU fabric, sub-30-min comp turnarounds

Review & Finishing

Review Loop drove notes from director to artists with frame-accurate timecoding. AI summarization condensed every review into a single artist-facing brief.

  • 17 review rounds across the project
  • Final color and conform in DaVinci Resolve · HDR + SDR
  • Mastered for theatrical, streaming, and social cuts
