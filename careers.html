Why Work Here

You'll touch
the frame.

Most studios silo their people. The director never sees the ML pipeline. The TD never sees the cut. The artist gets a brief and a deadline and that's it.

V01 is built the other way. Everyone at the studio gets close to the frame — the engineers ship inside real productions, the directors get to shape the tools, the artists get to break what doesn't work and rebuild it.

"Small team. Wide remit. Real frames on real screens."

If you want to do the work of three jobs, mostly because you can't help yourself, this is probably the place. Bring taste. We'll bring the rest.

How We Hire
01

Taste, Then Skill

Skill we can sharpen. Taste is harder. We hire for an eye, an ear, and a point of view — then we put real shots in front of you.

02

Generalists Welcome

If your résumé doesn't fit a single role we still want to hear from you. The best people we've hired draw on three or four disciplines.

03

Show The Work

Reels, GitHubs, journals, side projects, weird experiments — show us what you're already making, even if it's unfinished.

04

No Take-Home Theater

We don't ask for unpaid spec work. If we go deep on a problem together, we pay for your time.

05

Real Decisions, Fast

Two-week process from intro to offer. You'll meet the people you'd actually work with, on day one.

06

Fit Goes Both Ways

You'll spend time with the lab, the studio, and at least one director or supe. We want you to know what you're walking into.
